## External services & APIs

This plugin connects to several 3rd party services to provide crucial functionality. Each is listed below with what services are used and what information is shared, if any.

### Email and flow templates
Email and flow templates are pulled periodically from a remote repository via API at `https://library.groundhogg.io`. No information about you or your site is shared or logged in this request.

### Dashboard notices and widgets
Recent blog posts from `https://groundhogg.io` are shown in the dashboard and are fetched periodically. No data or information is logged or shared.
Important notifications and dashboard notices are pulled from `https://groundho.gg` periodically. No data or information is logged or shared.

### Telemetry and usage
You can opt-in and opt-out of site telemetry from Groundhogg » Settings » Misc » Danger Zone » Opt-in to anonymous usage tracking. When enabled, the following information will be periodically shared with `https://groundhogg.io`: Your admin email address; Whether your site has a valid and active license from `groundhogg.io`; The license if one exists; Whether Groundhogg is white labelled; System information such as WordPress version, PHP Version, the Groundhogg plugin version, the site time sone, and site language; Usage statistics such as the number of active flows, the number of new contacts, and the number of emails sent; The list of active Groundhogg add-ons on the site;
No information is shared or collected when telemetry sharing is disabled.

### Google Recaptcha
If you have the Google Recaptcha integration enabled, scripts will be loaded from `https://www.google.com/recaptcha/api.js` and requests will be made to `https://www.google.com/recaptcha/api/siteverify` sharing only essential information (such as your recaptcha site key) to facilitate the integration.

### Cloudflare Turnstile
If you have the Cloudflare Turnstile integration enabled, scripts will be loaded from `https://challenges.cloudflare.com/turnstile/v0/api.js` and requests will be made to `https://challenges.cloudflare.com/turnstile/v0/siteverify` sharing only essential information (such as your turnstile site key) to facilitate the integration.

### Geolocation services
Groundhogg will attempt (if licensed) to geolocate a contact (country, region, timezone, etc...) based on their IP address using our Geolocation service `https://ipinfo.groundhogg.io`. No data is stored remotely and no PII is used. We use a third party based in Romania to provide geolocation services.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is Groundhogg an AI CRM? =
Yes. AI agents like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Cursor connect to Groundhogg through MCP from the AI agent tab in Tools, then build flows, write emails, schedule broadcasts and manage contacts for you. They can only do what the WordPress user they connect as is allowed to do.

= Does Groundhogg need any additional marketing software like MailChimp? =
No, Groundhogg is a completely standalone, lightweight CRM and marketing automation system designed to save you $1,000s while helping you generate more revenue.

= Does Groundhogg require and external transactional email service like SendGrid? =
No, but it is **highly** recommended. Without a proper transactional email service your emails are likely to end up in spam.

Groundhogg provides a number of recommended solutions:

* [MailHawk](https://mailhawk.io/)
* [SendGrid](https://groundhogg.io/downloads/sendgrid-integration/)
* [AWS SES](https://groundhogg.io/downloads/aws/)
* [Elastic Email](https://groundhogg.io/downloads/elastic-email-integration/)
* [Basic SMTP](https://groundhogg.io/downloads/smtp/) for other services.

Groundhogg will automatically use the mail solution you have configured on your WordPress website.

= Does Groundhogg have any required monthly fee or upgrades? =
No, you can use Groundhogg CRM and Marketing Automation for free forever. Groundhogg is a Open Source project designed to democratize CRM and email marketing automation and provide simple easy to use tools to new and non-tech-savvy business owners.

That being said, our [premium upgrades](https://groundhogg.io/pricing/) can seriously help your business! Use our proven tools and strategies to supercharge your marketing and WordPress site!

= Does Groundhogg connect to Woocommerce or other plugins? =
Yes, Groundhogg has [integrations](https://groundhogg.io/downloads/category/integrations/) for the most popular WordPress plugins around to create an even more powerful CRM.

= Does Groundhogg work with my theme? =
Groundhogg is theme agnostic, meaning it will work with every and any theme. Groundhogg is mostly administrative and very little is seen by visitors.

= Does Groundhogg track my subscribers? =
Yes, Groundhogg has a very smart tracking system that will allow you to monitor your clients interactions with your emails and your site.

= Does Groundhogg Integrate with my membership plugin? =
Yes, Groundhogg has a builtin connection with the WordPress user API, meaning it will automatically use user roles in your site, like from a membership plugin.

= Is Groundhogg secure? =
We follow all best WordPress security practices. Plus it's open source, so we have a large community looking out for potential weaknesses when it comes to your data.

= Is Groundhogg GDPR, CANSPAM, or CASL compliant?
Yes, we have tools available to meet the criteria of all the worlds ANTI-SPAM & privacy legislation.

= Is Groundhogg better than my current CRM/Marketing Automation tool? =
Well, that's up to you! We'll never say Groundhogg is better than anyone, but it is **different.** And sometimes different is better than better.

= Where can I upgrade to premium? =
You can purchase a premium plan for access to support and our premium add-ons & integrations on our [pricing page](https://groundhogg.io/pricing/).

= How can I report security bugs? =
You can report security bugs through the Patchstack Vulnerability Disclosure Program. The Patchstack team helps validate, triage and handle any security vulnerabilities. [Report a security vulnerability.]( https://patchstack.com/database/vdp/9e5fb9d9-417e-4ba2-a0bf-8b7529b7122b )

== Changelog ==

= 4.11 (2026-11-05) =
* ADDED A `view_contact_pii` capability that hides contacts' personal information. Users without it, administrators included, can still work with contacts by ID, tags, activity, segments, broadcasts and flows, but see `[redacted]` in place of names, email addresses, phone numbers and other personal details, and can't search, sort or filter by them. Every role that can view contacts gets it on update, so nothing changes until you take it away.
 * It applies to the admin screens, the REST API and the AI abilities, so an AI agent connected as such a user is limited too. Exports, imports and contact files are closed to them. Add-ons redact the columns and cards they add.
 * A Personal information checkbox on a user's profile grants or revokes it, whatever their role. Only a user who has it can change it.
 * Personal details written in a note or a task are redacted, and custom fields marked Contains personal information are hidden.
* ADDED An opt-in daily My day email with a user's overdue and due tasks, unread replies and new contacts, sent at the time and in the timezone they choose. Tasks can be snoozed or completed from it without signing in, and a sales manager can add their team's. Turn it on from the Notifications card of the user profile.
* ADDED Every admin notification email has a signed link to unsubscribe from it without signing in.
* ADDED A dashboard recommendation to turn on the email summaries.
* ADDED The `groundhogg/edit-email` ability changes part of an email instead of rewriting all of it. In a block email it replaces text, changes a block's content or settings, and adds, moves or deletes blocks. In an HTML email it replaces visible text or an exact piece of HTML. Changes are all or nothing, with `dry_run` and `expected_version`.
* ADDED The `groundhogg/send-test-email` ability sends a design test of a saved email, and the email abilities return a `preview_url`.
* TWEAKED `groundhogg/get-email-template` returns an outline of a block email, or the visible text of an HTML email, instead of the whole body. `blocks`, `find`, `view` "specs" and `view` "full" return more.
* TWEAKED `groundhogg/create-email-template` and `groundhogg/update-email-template` accept `blocks`, each described by its type and settings, and write the same markup the email editor would. Block content the editor couldn't open is refused.
* TWEAKED `groundhogg/send-email-template`, `groundhogg/add-to-flow` and `groundhogg/simulate-flow` accept `event_args`, so replacement codes that depend on them resolve.
* SECURITY `{post_title}` could output unescaped HTML from a post title saved by a Contributor. Credit: Wordfence PRISM, via Wordfence.
* FIXED Excluding several tags only excluded contacts that had all of them, and excluding contacts with all of the tags excluded contacts with any one of them.
* FIXED Link Click redirects could be stored by a page cache or CDN, which skipped the trigger, and didn't recognize visitors on a site that answers on both `example.com` and `www.example.com`.
* FIXED Saving in the email editor changed blocks that hadn't been edited, giving links the Arial font and full width contained emails another wrapper table each time. It also replaced changes made while the editor was open, like by an AI agent, and now asks first.
* FIXED `groundhogg/send-email-broadcast` ignored `exclude_segment`, and its `estimated_recipients` counted contacts that are skipped.
* FIXED A single step, form submission or page visit couldn't be read, edited or deleted through the REST API, even by administrators.
* FIXED The event queue and background tasks put a 60 second limit on processes that had none, like a server cron job.
* FIXED The AI agent tab said the MCP Adapter plugin was active when another plugin loaded its own copy of it.
* FIXED Incoming Messages couldn't be turned on for a white labeled subsite that uses the main site's licenses.
* FIXED Items added with the `groundhogg/admin/checklist_items` and `groundhogg/admin/recommendation_items` filters weren't shown, and the profile of a sales manager who can't delete users raised an error.
* DEV `Groundhogg\Pii` is the policy for contact personal information, with `Pii::is_restricted()`, `Pii::REDACTED`, `Pii::restricted_error()`, `Pii::scrub_text()`, helpers to redact a screen and `Groundhogg.functions.contactLabel()` in JavaScript. Extend it with the `groundhogg/pii/is_restricted`, `contact_fields`, `meta_keys`, `safe_meta_keys`, `filter_types`, `scrub_text_pairs` and `table_columns` filters. A custom field is personal when `pii` is set.
* DEV `Main_Roles::map_meta_cap()` maps the step, submission and page visit capabilities.
* DEV `Email_Block_Document` and `Email_Block_Renderer` parse, edit and render block email content. Add-on block types register with the `groundhogg/email/block_types` and `groundhogg/email/block_renderers` filters.
* DEV The `groundhogg/email_reports/my_day_sections` filter adds sections to My day.
* DEV The REST API's email update accepts `version` and refuses a stale one with a `groundhogg_email_changed` 409 error.

Older versions are in the full changelog on [groundhogg.io](https://groundhogg.io/changelog/).
